# Terms of use

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

Schierami provides informational assistance for fantasy soccer lineup decisions.
Recommendations are estimates under uncertainty, not guarantees of goals, points,
rankings, prizes or victory. Data coverage and tool availability vary by host and
competition.

## Responsibility and limitations

Users remain responsible for checking league rules, roster eligibility and the
submission deadline. When a missing rule can change the advice, the skill should
ask for that rule or make its recommendation conditional.

The optional calculators cover only their documented contracts. They are not
complete replicas of any fantasy platform. The skill does not submit lineups or
change league settings automatically.

## Third-party information and access

News, official announcements and statistics may be incomplete, delayed or wrong.
Mentioning a provider does not imply endorsement. Use only available, authorized
access methods; do not bypass authentication or share private credentials in chat.
The current Leghe Fantacalcio adapter does not depend on automatic league-page
scraping or undocumented authenticated APIs.
