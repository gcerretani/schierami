# Source routing, not a mandatory website checklist

Choose sources by question, competition coverage, freshness and actual access. Keep the claim-level ledger defined in research-protocol.md. A provider name is not a permanent guarantee of coverage or quality. Do not claim a source was used because it appears here.

| Need | Prefer | Fallback / cautions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roster and private settings | User files, screenshots, statements, authorized league access | Preserve provenance; public defaults do not establish private selections. |
| Fixtures, suspensions, official XI | Competition and club official channels | Specialist report that identifies the original announcement. |
| Probable XI and expected minutes | Current fantasy/team reporting plus independent corroboration when decisive | Fantacalcio.it and Sky Sport for relevant Italian coverage; use local equivalents elsewhere. |
| Role and set pieces | Recent actual usage, coach comments, reliable tactical reports | Historical hierarchies are priors after transfers or coach changes. |
| Minutes, shots, chance quality | Established event-data provider with definitions and current coverage | FotMob, FBref when the metric is available, competition/team data; verify competition and sample. |
| Rules | User's selected settings plus the relevant provider's official specification | Private/offline rules can override a preset; clarify conflicts. |
| Scientific method | Original papers and explicit evidence-to-rule mapping | Do not use a paper as evidence of a player's current availability. |

Use raw comparable components, not averages of opaque provider ratings. Keep editorial votes separate from event-model ratings. Do not treat xG as a guarantee. Odds can inform game-state priors, not starting status.

Avoid paywall or login bypass and undocumented authenticated endpoints. Use permitted access methods; provider restrictions belong in the relevant adapter, not as bans on all platforms.

For unfamiliar competitions, first find official schedule/team sources and local reporting. If evidence is thin, ask for useful user-held information and lower confidence. Do not substitute Serie A players, scoring or fixtures automatically.

Currentness and provenance matter more than collecting a fixed number of links.
