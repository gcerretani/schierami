# Mantra: positions, combinations and substitutions

## Intake and legality

Obtain the competition's current role list and permitted tactical schemas. Map every starter to a position, not merely a broad D/C/A group. Multiple eligible roles do not make every position in every schema legal. Read conditional position restrictions and adaptation rules; never invent a compatibility matrix.

Collect Basic/Easy/Master, bench order and size, substitution cap, goalkeeper priority, adaptation penalties and any enabled factors or custom rules. Do not import the Classic defense modifier, captain or scoring defaults.

## Substitution reasoning

According to the official reference below, Basic prioritizes the original schema without penalties, then other penalty-free schemas, then permitted adaptations. Easy keeps the original schema. Master prioritizes bench order among feasible penalty-free schemas rather than giving priority to the submitted schema; adapted solutions are a later tier. These summaries do not replace the complete algorithm.

Evaluate absent outfield players together and respect bench-combination priority. Reassign remaining starters as well as entering reserves where permitted. A one-at-a-time same-role loop is not the official Mantra engine. Account separately for goalkeeper handling and its consumption of the substitution allowance.

Show the recommended XI by slot and the bench in actual preference order, not an automatic P/D/C/A grouping. Check at least the consequential multiple-absence cases. D-Factor/R-Factor and adaptations require their own confirmed formulas.

## Tool boundary

The bundled validator checks explicitly supplied role-slot tables. It does not verify the authenticity of those tables or enforce every conditional Mantra schema restriction. The scorer's ordered_slots primitive is NOT Basic, Easy or Master. Do not rename a Mantra mode to make the script accept it.

For official substitution resolution use a verified complete manual derivation or an available authorized platform simulator; otherwise state the uncertainty and give conditional cover. Ask for the exact schema/rule or a small example if needed, not the entire roster again. Unsupported automation does not mean unsupported assistance.
