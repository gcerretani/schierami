# Decision-first research

## Pass A: frame before searching

Recover scoped roster and rules; resolve decisive private gaps. Identify plausible legal lineups and normally 2-5 swing decisions. List the smallest public unknown that could reverse each. Check basic availability for all realistic starters but do not research unusable reserves as deeply as close choices.

## Pass B: resolve and stop

Research availability/minutes, conditional tactical role and set pieces, fixture context, then repeatable involvement as needed. This is an order of information collection, NOT a lexicographic player ranking. More minutes do not always beat better expected production and legal bench insurance.

Maintain a compact evidence ledger for consequential facts: player/team, claim, source/locator, publication or update time, season/matchday, independence/origin, observed fact versus inference, confidence and affected decision. Do not dump it into every answer. It must allow an honest answer to "which sources did you use?"

A search-result snippet or a link written in the response is not proof the full page was read. Mark inaccessible/unverified details accordingly.

Compare timestamps, resolve copied stories to their origin, and prefer direct confirmation for factual status. Official squad selection does not prove a start or 90-minute fitness. Official XI overrides probable XI only for that same match. Do not treat a site header's date as an article update. Verify season, fixture, player identity, club and competition; a prior-round lineup is only prior evidence.

Search for facts, not generic recommendations. Example query patterns: `[player] [club] [opponent] starting injury [date]`, `[club] squad press conference`, `[player] minutes shots xG xA [competition season]`. Adapt language to the league.

## Freshness and efficiency

Within six hours of the deadline prefer same-day decisive news. At 6-24 hours use latest meaningful reports; beyond 24 hours label prospective lineups preliminary. Returns from injury, coach/role changes and unexpected omissions require fresher checks.

Reuse fixture and team information across players. Cache with scope and as-of time, not indefinitely. Official XI can close an availability question alone. Otherwise seek independent corroboration for consequential disputed claims; do not count syndicated copies. No compulsory tour of every source in sources.md.

Stop when an authoritative source resolves the fact, independent current evidence converges without credible contradiction, or plausible residual uncertainty cannot change the decision. Do not gather low-value statistics to avoid asking a rule.

## Forecast discipline and fallback

Use short windows for role/minutes changes, medium windows for involvement and longer samples for skill. Never compare per-90 rates without sample/minutes context. Do not fabricate metrics or convert probable-XI percentages into calibrated odds.

If a provider lacks coverage, try a suitable alternative or use a qualitative comparison. Name the remaining uncertainty and the news that would flip the call. Without live research, provide supported provisional advice rather than refusing, but do not claim freshness. Without code, do not claim scripts or simulations ran. Cite only sources actually consulted for the claims they support.
