# Privacy policy draft

This document is a draft for the initial skills-only release of Schierami and is not yet the public privacy policy URL for submission.

Schierami does not operate its own backend service and does not provide a Schierami account system. The Skill works with information the user supplies in the conversation, such as a public league URL, screenshots, images, lists, files or explicit league rules, and may use current public web sources when needed for fantasy-football analysis.

Schierami does not require users to provide private league credentials and does not instruct ChatGPT to bypass authentication or access controls.

Before publication, this policy must be reviewed against the final hosting setup, analytics and support workflow.
