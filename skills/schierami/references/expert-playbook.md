# Evidence-informed decision checklist

Use the entrypoint's workflow; do not run a second duplicate research process. Evidence IDs refer to scientific-evidence.md. Calculation semantics and script limits belong in scoring-model.md, not in an assumed platform preset.

## Before deciding

- Scope the game, exact relevant rules and objective. [D1]
- Find decision-changing gaps before researching extra player statistics.
- Verify start/appearance/no-vote scenarios, then combine them with production and legal replacements. Research order is not a strict player ranking. [E3, D3]
- Update a longer-run prior using current role, minutes and opportunity. Do not chase one isolated bonus or mistake a familiar name for evidence. [E4, E7, E12]
- Consider real role and opponent mechanisms, not merely favorite status. [E8, E9]
- Treat xG and consensus as uncertain inputs; distinguish reports from independent confirmation, and do not average incompatible ratings. [E6, E10, E11]
- Check individual rotation/fitness under congestion, not a universal malus. [E13]

## Whole-lineup comparison

Compare all realistically competitive legal modules, not only habitual formations. Consider valid-vote combinations, replacement order, nonlinear bonuses, captaincy, locks and opponent-dependent rules. Avoid adding replacement value twice. [E2, D2]

A fourth defender is not automatically best just because a modifier is enabled. Stable votes are not universally superior near a threshold. Use variance only in relation to the chosen objective and plausible scenarios, not as an aesthetic preference for safety or differentials. [D1, D2]

For Mantra consider slot placement and combined substitutions. For a custom rule, clarify precedence with an example. A supported partial answer beats fabricated certainty; clearly separate the fixed core from rule-dependent choices. [E14]

## Before sending

Check legality, coverage and evidence for the decisive claims. Explain the actual tie-breaker and what would change it. Avoid claims such as "scientifically optimal" when only qualitative comparisons were performed. A source list is not proof that its contents were read or used.

## After matches

Review information available before the deadline: availability assumptions, role, opportunities, rule interpretation and bench behavior. Keep outcome and process separate. Evaluate over batches, with prediction calibration only for quantities actually predicted. One lucky goal does not validate a method. [E1, E5, E12]
