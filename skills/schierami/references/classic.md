# Classic and role-group formats

Use the competition's actual role list, not a player's club position. P/D/C/A are common labels, not mandatory for a custom competition. Obtain permitted modules and bench limits instead of treating 3-4-3 and 4-3-3 as the only candidates.

Separate submitted lineup, valid-vote players and effective lineup after changes. Check no-vote exceptions before deciding someone is replaceable. A substitute's brief appearance with a valid poor vote can prevent a better reserve entering.

For substitutions obtain maximum changes, goalkeeper priority, same-role versus module-changing behavior, ordering when multiple players are absent, and office reserves. Traditional/Dynamic/Hybrid are platform-specific labels: translate the actual published algorithm, not a remembered name. Switch is not simply an extra substitution and must be interpreted under that league's rules.

For every modifier obtain activation count, goalkeeper inclusion, selected votes, base versus fantasy vote, thresholds and boundary inclusivity, rounding, timing and whether the effect changes one's own or the opponent's score. Do not transfer a default table between providers. Formation-dependent effects may change after substitutions; clean-sheet probability is not the same as expected editorial vote.
